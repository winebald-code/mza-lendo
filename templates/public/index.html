The gap

A workshop owner already knows what is wrong. Finding out costs them the afternoon.

The information exists. It is in a supervisor's head, a notebook by the grinder, a pile of delivery notes and three WhatsApp groups. Pulling it together means walking the floor and asking. By the time you know the steel ran out, the run is already late.

A number is useless without the reason behind it.
