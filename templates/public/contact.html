One inbox, answered by the people who build the thing. There is no ticket queue to disappear into and no chatbot in front of it.

Email

info@winebald.tech — for everything below. We answer within one working day, Nairobi time, and usually sooner.

If your floor is stopped

Put URGENT at the front of the subject line and include your plant name. A stopped line is not a support ticket, and it does not wait its turn.

Before you write, two things save an hour:

  • Check service status — if the USSD line is down it is already known.
  • Have a worker dial the shortcode from a second network, so we know which side the fault is on.

Questions before signing up

Tell us what the plant makes, roughly how many people report on a shift, and what you are using now — a book, a spreadsheet, or nothing. That is enough for a straight answer about whether Bacaan fits, including when it does not.

Something wrong with your data

A worker's record, a stock figure you did not enter, a run that looks wrong. Send the plant name and roughly when it happened. Every change is written to an audit trail, so a mistaken entry can usually be traced back to the session that made it.

Security reports

If you have found a vulnerability, write to the same address with SECURITY in the subject and please give us time to fix it before publishing. We will confirm receipt, tell you what we found, and credit you if you want that. See security for what the application already does.

Data requests

Access, correction, deletion or an export of what is held about you. Same address, and see the privacy policy for what those rights cover.

Where we are

Winebald Technologies, Nairobi, Kenya. Working hours are 08:00 to 17:00 East Africa Time, Monday to Friday. Urgent floor outages are read outside them.
