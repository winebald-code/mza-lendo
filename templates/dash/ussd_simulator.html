Why USSD and not an app

0 MB

No data needed. The session runs on the signalling channel.

Any

Works on the cheapest handset on the floor. No install, no updates.

<30s

Short enough that people actually do it, which is the whole point.

This simulator posts to /ussd/callback — the same endpoint Africa's Talking calls in production. There is no separate demo path, so anything you record here shows up in the ledger, the schedule and the Pulse exactly as a real session would.
